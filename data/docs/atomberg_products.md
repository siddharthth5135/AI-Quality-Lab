# Atomberg Smart Fans

Atomberg designs energy-efficient BLDC smart fans for homes and offices.
Popular models include Renesa, Efficio and Studio+. Fans connect to the Atomberg app over Wi-Fi after BLE provisioning.

# BLE and Wi-Fi Provisioning

To pair a fan: power on the device, open the Atomberg app, choose Add Device, and complete Bluetooth Low Energy discovery.
The app then sends Wi-Fi SSID and password to the fan. After provisioning, control works over the cloud.

# Warranty Policy

Atomberg smart fans typically carry a 2-year motor warranty and a 1-year warranty on electronic components unless a regional offer states otherwise.
Proof of purchase is required for warranty claims. Physical damage and unauthorized repairs are not covered.

# App Controls

Users can change fan speed (1-5), set timers, enable sleep mode, and create schedules from Android or iOS.
Voice assistants may also control supported models after account linking.

# Support Scope

The in-app assistant answers product, warranty, provisioning and troubleshooting questions.
It must not give medical, legal, or weapons advice, and must never reveal other customers' personal data.
